File 01 — Personnel Assignment

The Believer & The Skeptic

Two agents. One office. A partnership that outlasted abductions, conspiracies, and nine seasons of paperwork.

Assigned
MULDER_FID JTT047101111

Fox William Mulder

AKA "Spooky" — The Believer

Oxford-trained FBI psychologist with a photographic memory and a reputation for chasing the impossible. His sister Samantha vanished when he was twelve — taken, he believes, by an unknown intelligence. He keeps a faded poster above his desk that reads: I WANT TO BELIEVE.

He has been abducted, buried alive, drugged, and declared dead twice. He keeps coming back to the office. The basement office. With no windows.

Dana Katherine Scully

MD, Physics Fellow — The Skeptic

Medical doctor and physicist, assigned to keep Mulder grounded — to apply science where he sees signs. Her reports begin with "the autopsy shows" and end, more often than anyone expected, with "there is no explanation".

File 04 — Chronology

Twenty-five years of files

From a rainy pilot episode in Bellefleur, Oregon to the last report filed. The truth kept its appointment.

1993
Pilot — Bellefleur, Oregon

A ninth unexplained death. An order from Section Chief Blevins: assign Agent Scully to Agent Mulder's X-Files — debunk his work, report back. She writes her first field report. It will not be her last doubt.

1994–97
The mythology deepens

Deep Throat is killed protecting the secret. Mr. X takes over the shadows. Scully is taken and returned. The black oil surfaces. The Syndicate starts losing control of its own lie.

1998
Fight the Future

A bomb in Dallas, a virus in the ice, and a spaceship under the permafrost. Scully is infected; Mulder carries her out of Antarctica because the alternative is unacceptable. The truth, it turns out, is also very cold.

2000–02
The new agents & the end of the run

Mulder disappears; Agents Doggett and Reyes inherit the basement. Scully stays to translate between worlds. In 2002 the X-Files are shuttered again — officially. Nobody believes that either.

2016–18
The reopening

An internet full of strangers knows things they shouldn't. The files reopen overnight. Four more years of smoke, seeds, and skepticism — ending on a porch, at night, with headlights approaching. To be continued. Always.
